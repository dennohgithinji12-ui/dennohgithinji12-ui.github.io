From Virgin Soil to Finished Reality.
Single-Contract Accountability.

In Kenya, the typical build suffers from friction between the land agent, architect, quantity surveyor, structural engineer, and subcontractors. We replace that with a unified turnkey system orchestrated by Raphael Kamau with fixed budgets and guaranteed timelines.

Phase 01 • Weeks 1 to 4

Land Scouting, Geotechnical Due Diligence & Purchase

We identify ideal parcels in Nairobi, Kiambu, Kwale, or Nakuru County. Raphael conducts physical site inspections, soil core borings, and coordinates Ardhisasa title searches. We execute purchase contracts with advocate escrow protection.

Ardhisasa Title Search Survey Beacon Resurvey Geotechnical Soil Profiles Advocate Conveyancing
Phase 02 • Weeks 5 to 10

3D BIM Architectural Design & Statutory Permitting

We produce precision Building Information Modeling (BIM) architectural models and photorealistic 3D VR simulations. Our structural engineers complete load calculations, electrical schematics, and plumbing layouts. We secure all approvals from Nairobi City County, NEMA, and the National Construction Authority (NCA).

3D Architectural BIM Models NEMA Environmental Impact Assessment County Architectural & Structural Approvals NCA Project Registration
Phase 03 • Months 3 to 5

Precision Earthworks & Engineered Substructure

Site grading, laser-level excavation, and rock-breaking. We engineer custom foundation footings tailored to local conditions: black-cotton replacement, volcanic rock anchors in Tigoni, or deep marine piles in coastal Diani. Subterranean utility lines and water retention tanks are cast into concrete.

Laser-Guided Grading Deep Concrete Geo-Piles Black Cotton Mitigation Water Storage Cisterns
Phase 04 • Months 6 to 10

Structural Steel Frame, Slabs & Weatherproof Envelope

Heavy structural steel columns, cantilevered post-tensioned slabs, and reinforced machine-cut stonework. Floor-to-ceiling thermally-broken glass curtain walls are installed alongside waterproof membrane roofing, acoustic insulation, and solar mounting structures.

Structural Steel & Slabs Triple-Glazed Curtain Walls High-Performance Roof Membrane Solar PV Integration
Phase 05 • Months 11 to 14

Master Finishes, Smart Tech & White-Glove Handover

High-end interior architecture: custom mvule & teak millwork, Italian porcelain, Lutron smart lighting, solar inverter commissioning, swimming pool filtration, and manicured landscaping. Raphael completes the 120-point quality audit, secures the County Certificate of Occupancy, and hands over keys with a 10-year warranty.

Custom Architectural Millwork Smart-Home Automation County Certificate of Occupancy 10-Year Structural Warranty
