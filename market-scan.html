Strategic Sourcing

Affordable & High-Growth Land Corridors

Looking to buy land with high capital appreciation and accessible build budgets? These emerging satellite hubs offer freehold title deeds, road infrastructure, and excellent value.

Kangundo Road Corridor

+22% YoY
Joska • Malaa • Kamulu • Kantafu

The fastest-growing budget suburban belt East of Nairobi. Fully dualized highway access, gentle flat terrain, expanding electricity grid, and low foundation earthwork costs.

50x100 (1/8th Acre) Plot:
KSh 650,000 – KSh 1,800,000
1 Full Acre: KSh 2.8M – KSh 6.0M

Kitengela & Isinya Axis

+18% YoY
Namanga Highway • Acacia • Isinya Plains

Directly connected to Nairobi CBD via Nairobi Expressway. Established international schools, private hospitals, piped water, and rapid gated-community development.

50x100 (1/8th Acre) Plot:
KSh 850,000 – KSh 2,200,000
1 Full Acre: KSh 3.5M – KSh 7.5M

Juja Farm & Ruiru East

+20% YoY
Thika Superhighway • Eastern Bypass

Tarmacked Juja Farm road has unlocked incredible affordable development land. High rental yield potential, fertile red soil, and strong connectivity to universities.

50x100 (1/8th Acre) Plot:
KSh 1,200,000 – KSh 2,800,000
1 Full Acre: KSh 8.0M – KSh 16.0M

Naivasha & Mai Mahiu

+15% YoY
Longonot • SGR Inland Depot • Gilgil

Pristine mountain and crater views, SGR passenger terminus, holiday home capital. Affordable volcanic soil with superior bearing capacity for foundation works.

50x100 (1/8th Acre) Plot:
KSh 600,000 – KSh 1,500,000
1 Full Acre: KSh 2.2M – KSh 5.5M

Matuu, Tala & Machakos

+14% YoY
Thika-Garissa Rd • Yatta Canal Belt

Unbeatable price per acre. Ideal for large residential homesteads, agro-compounds, or retirement retreats with easy water access and zero congestion.

50x100 (1/8th Acre) Plot:
KSh 450,000 – KSh 950,000
1 Full Acre: KSh 1.2M – KSh 3.0M

Coast Budget Corridors

+16% YoY
Malindi Inland • Kilifi Mnarani • Mariakani

Second-row coastal plots 5 to 10 minutes from the beach. Affordable coral bedrock, coastal lifestyle, and prime holiday Airbnb demand at a fraction of beachfront prices.

50x100 (1/8th Acre) Plot:
KSh 450,000 – KSh 1,400,000
1 Full Acre: KSh 1.8M – KSh 4.5M
