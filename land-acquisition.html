Rigorous Kenyan Protocol

The 6-Point Land Due Diligence Framework

Over 420+ acres scouted and legally acquired without a single title dispute or ownership encumbrance.

📜

Ministry of Lands Search

Official digital and registry verification on Ardhisasa. We confirm registered proprietors, check for active caveats, encumbrances, bank charges, or green-card disputes.

📍

Cadastral Beacon Resurvey

Licensed Survey of Kenya cartographers resurvey and verify all boundary beacons using high-precision RTK GPS. We eliminate boundary overlap with neighbors.

🔬

Geotechnical Soil Mechanics

Engineered soil borings identify red soil, murram depth, black cotton layers, or volcanic rock. Raphael calculates exact foundation costs before you close the purchase.

🏛️

County Zoning & Setbacks

Verification with Nairobi City County, Kiambu, Kwale, or Nakuru County governments regarding building height limits, density ratios, and road reserve setbacks.

💧

Utilities & Water Feasibility

Hydrogeological survey for borehole drilling potential, high-voltage Kenya Power (KPLC) transformer capacity, and municipal sewer or biodigester feasibility.

⚖️

Advocate Escrow Closing

All financial transactions flow through verified bank escrow accounts handled by top conveyancing advocates. Funds are only released upon genuine title transfer.
